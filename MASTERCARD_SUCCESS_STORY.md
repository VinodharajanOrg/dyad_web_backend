# Mastercard Vibe Success Story

**The Problem:** A global financial services company was drowning in requests from business teams who had great ideas but no way to bring them to life quickly. Product managers and innovation teams needed working demos to show leadership and get buy-in before spending months on development. But the traditional approach—submitting IT tickets and waiting weeks for prototypes—was killing momentum. Great ideas were getting shelved, market opportunities were slipping away, and teams were wasting time building features that executives would reject anyway. And when it came time to present to decision-makers, people were stuck with static slides that couldn't convey the real potential of their ideas or show data in meaningful ways.

**The Solution Delivered:** Apexon partnered with Mastercard to solve this head-on. We started with a simple proof of concept: what if business users could just describe what they wanted in plain English, and AI would build it for them? It worked beautifully. Over twelve weeks, we built Vibe—a no-code platform where anyone can generate working applications in minutes without writing a single line of code. We integrated Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers so people could create beautiful dashboards on the fly, added one-click sharing so prototypes could be sent to anyone globally, and made sure everything ran securely within Mastercard's infrastructure. Product managers and innovation teams got their hands on it first. The response? They loved it. Now we're planning the next phase and getting ready to roll it out across the organization.

**How Apexon's AI Work Drove Better Client Outcomes**

**Faster stakeholder approvals:** Instead of endless PowerPoint presentations, business leaders now walk into meetings with actual working applications. Approval cycles that used to take months now happen in days.

**Reduced wasted development investment:** Teams validate their ideas with real prototypes before committing serious development resources. If an idea doesn't resonate with stakeholders, they find out immediately—not after months of expensive development.

**Empowered non-technical innovation:** Product managers, business analysts, and strategists can now create and test their ideas without waiting for IT or learning to code. Innovation isn't bottlenecked by technical resources anymore.

**Compelling data-driven presentations:** With MCP integration, anyone can generate professional dashboards with charts and analytics in minutes. Business presentations went from "trust me, this will work" to "here's the data showing exactly how it works."

**Instant global collaboration:** Share a secure link and your colleagues in London, Singapore, or Mumbai can test your prototype immediately. No complicated setup, no installation—just click and go.

**Scalable innovation model:** The organization can now test 10x more ideas without hiring more developers. Good ideas move forward fast, and weak ideas get filtered out early.

**Foundation for broader digital transformation:** With Vibe up and running, Mastercard now has a proven platform for rapid innovation that can grow with them—setting the stage for future capabilities and organization-wide adoption.
